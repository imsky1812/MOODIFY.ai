What is MOODIFY.ai?

MOODIFY.ai is a premium emotional wellbeing companion designed to act as your acoustic sanctuary. It leverages advanced facial expressions, voice acoustics, and sentiment reasoning to gauge your current state of mind and dynamically calibrate acoustic therapy using Spotify's library.

How to use it

  • Authenticate: Sign in with Google to establish your private wellbeing profile.
  • Calibrate: Enable your camera or microphone, or simply chat to describe your day.
  • Acoustic Therapy: Tap companion triggers to generate dynamic Spotify play recommendations matched to your state.

Mental Health & Music Healing

Music has a direct neurobiological impact on mental health. Sound waves stimulate the autonomic nervous system, regulating cortisol (stress hormone) levels and increasing dopamine production.
